# CHARACTER_DESIGN.md

## Visual direction
The first cast translates an NES-era readable silhouette philosophy into simple 3D rather than copying any NES character.

Shared goals:
- clear silhouette on a phone screen
- low polygon counts
- flat/vertex-color palette
- exaggerated proportions
- separate named body parts for later rigging
- no dependence on high-resolution textures

## Player: Aster
Role: player adventurer.

Design cues:
- navy field coat
- orange scarf
- asymmetric gray-blue shoulder guard
- compact backpack and bedroll
- side pouch
- simple dark hair
- no pointed cap, plumber cap, Nintendo costume, or copied silhouette

Gameplay intent:
- readable running/jumping silhouette
- room for later sword/tool/item animations
- backpack visually supports an exploration theme

## Ground enemy: Moss Mite
Role: basic ground enemy.

Design cues:
- low squat beetle body
- layered stone shell with moss growth
- amber eyes
- six legs
- two forward feelers

Gameplay intent:
- patrol/charge enemy
- readable from top-down and third-person angles
- shell can later communicate armored/hit-state mechanics

## Flying enemy: Prismwing
Role: basic flying enemy.

Design cues:
- violet insect-like body
- cyan crystal wings
- narrow hanging tail
- antennae
- bright cyan eyes

Gameplay intent:
- hovering/patrol/dive behavior
- obvious air silhouette
- wings can animate independently

## Neutral NPC: Waykeeper
Role: reusable civilian/traveler base.

Design cues:
- teal outer garment
- gray-blue cowl
- cream undershirt
- diagonal satchel strap
- brown trousers/boots

Gameplay intent:
- base for merchant, traveler, guide, and quest-giver variants
- visually distinct from the player

## Current asset status
All four are CANDIDATE static mesh designs. Rigging and animation are a later Phase 1C task. User visual approval is required before the designs are treated as visually verified.
